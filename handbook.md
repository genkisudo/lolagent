## Multi-kill definition

A multi-kill occurs when the same champion kills multiple enemy champions, with each kill occurring within 10 seconds of the previous kill. Each new kill refreshes that 10-second window. After a Quadra Kill, the player has up to 30 seconds to secure the fifth kill for a Pentakill, provided that no enemy champion respawns during that extended window.

The important nuance is “within 10 seconds of the previous kill”, not necessarily that all kills must happen within one single 10-second period.

Example: `Kill 1 → 9 sec → Kill 2 → 9 sec → Kill 3 → 9 sec → Kill 4` is a Quadra Kill because every consecutive kill occurs within the refreshed 10-second window.
